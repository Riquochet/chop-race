About the race, and how to read the charts

Twenty teams started the season, and every week the lowest scorer is chopped: out of the league, its roster dumped onto the waiver wire. Still standing: —. This is the week's horse race, every surviving team's points from Thursday's kickoff to Monday's final whistle. Whoever is at the bottom when Monday night ends gets the axe; Chop% is each team's chance of being that team.

Updates itself while games are on. Solid lines are points scored; dashed lines are our projection to the end of Monday night, paced like real game time. A team's line is thick while one of its starters is playing, thin between its games, and hair-thin once it is done for the week. Friday, Saturday and the gaps between game windows are cut out. Final and Chop read ours (Sleeper).

Tap teams or players (their names, or rows in the tables) to pick out their lines; tap as many as you like, tap one again to drop it, or tap anywhere else to clear. Phone: slide one finger to read every score; pinch to zoom in on any spot, and drag with two fingers to move around. Mouse: hover to read, drag to scroll, pinch the trackpad to zoom. Double-tap to zoom out.

Players: every player on the team you pick. The very thick part of each line is his game (solid = scored play by play, dashed = projected to the final whistle); the very thin part is time he is off the field. Bench players are faded.
